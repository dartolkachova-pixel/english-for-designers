# Today I learned

Recently, I started thinking about how people often say, “Live in the moment.”
And it made me wonder: is living one day at a time actually a good way to live?

I think living one day at a time is actually very important.
Not because we should ignore the future, but because sometimes people forget to enjoy their lives right now.

A lot of us are always stressed about what comes next. Exams, work, money, plans, responsibilities. Of course, thinking about the future matters. We need goals, stability, and something to work toward.

But I think life becomes much happier when we can balance both things: enjoying the present while still building our future.

For example, I think it is important to appreciate small moments. Spending time with friends, drinking coffee slowly, listening to music, laughing at something stupid, or just having a peaceful day. These moments seem ordinary, but they actually make life feel real.

At the same time, I do not believe that “living in the moment” means being careless.
If we only think about today and never about tomorrow, we can create problems for ourselves later.

So for me, the best mindset is simple: enjoy the moment, but do not forget about your future self.

I think we should allow ourselves to be happy now while also doing things that will help us later. Because life is not only about surviving for the future. It is also about experiencing the present.
